Our Story

Style for Everyone

Shashi Collection is Thane's go-to store for men's fashion and everyday essentials, located in Daighar Gaon. We stock a wide range of casual and formal shirts, polos, round neck t-shirts, vests and caps, along with jeans, formal pants, baggy fit, straight fit, shorts and track pants. Beyond clothing, we're your one-stop shop for daily use essentials — innerwear, blankets, pillows, bedsheets, mosquito nets, towels, socks and handkerchiefs — plus gym bags, school bags and trolley bags for every need.
